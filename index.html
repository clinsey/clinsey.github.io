Welcome to my academic profile

Dr. Collins Sey

CyberSecurity | Machine Learning | Deep Learning

Dr. Collins Sey is an accomplished computer science expert with over a decade of combined industry and academic experience. He holds a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Cape Coast, Ghana, and both a Master's degree and a Ph.D. in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. Dr. Sey's expertise spans a wide range of cutting-edge fields, including Cybersecurity, Blockchain Technology, the Internet of Things (IoT), Smart City Innovations, Systems Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence. Dr. Sey has authored and co-authored several research publications in reputable journals and international conferences. He leverages his expertise, combined with a proven track record in industry and research to inspire and educate future computing professionals.
