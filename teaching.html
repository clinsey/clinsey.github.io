Teaching

Courses, workshops, and educational philosophy

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire."

My teaching philosophy centers on fostering critical thinking, practical problem-solving skills, and a deep understanding of fundamental concepts in computer science and software engineering. I believe in creating an inclusive learning environment where students are encouraged to question, experiment, and collaborate.

By integrating real-world industry experiences with academic rigor, I aim to prepare students not just for exams, but for successful careers in technology. My approach emphasizes hands-on learning, project-based assessments, and continuous feedback to ensure every student reaches their full potential.

Current Courses

Active courses for the current academic year

CHC5226

Foundation of Security

36 hours 250 students Spring 2025

Covers core principles of computer and information security, covering threat models, cryptographic foundations, and secure system design. Students apply security techniques to analyze vulnerabilities, implement secure solutions, and evaluate real-world systems within ethical and professional contexts.

Cryptography Network Security Secure Systems Vulnerability Analysis
CHC4906

Basic Communications and PC Networking

36 hours 250 students Spring 2025

Introduces fundamental principles of computer networking, including layered architectures, protocols, and IP addressing. Students design, configure, and test networks using routers and switches, with hands-on experience in network simulation and analysis tools.

Networking Fundamentals TCP/IP IP Addressing Subnetting Network Configuration
CHC5054

Web Application Development

36 hours 250 students Fall 2024

Covers the design and development of modern web applications, including front-end and server-side technologies. Students build and deploy applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Flask, and databases, with emphasis on accessibility, security, and scalable web architecture..

Web Development Flask Databases Web Security Server-Side Systems
